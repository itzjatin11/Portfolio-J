I can't leave a broken process alone.

Give me something clunky, manual, or held together with workarounds, and I want to understand why it works that way, then make it not need me to keep doing it by hand.

That instinct started before I wrote a line of code: chasing precision in cash operations, reading a room during a rush shift. Now, as a Business Analyst, I map how things really work, find where they break down, and build something better. I'd rather ship something small that works than talk about something big that might.
