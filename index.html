01 About

I'm a developer in Auckland who likes the unglamorous parts of software: clean data models, predictable APIs, and automation that removes busywork. I'd rather ship a smaller thing that holds up than a bigger one that breaks in week two.

Right now I'm a Business Analyst at Authentic Security, working on process improvement — requirements, process mapping and data analysis. It sharpens the question that comes before any code: what problem are we solving, and for whom?

Before that I built backend services for a video streaming app at Source Infinity, and finished a BIT in Software Development at the Auckland Institute of Studies with an Academic Excellence Award.
